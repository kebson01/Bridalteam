# Tells, and what to write instead

`scripts/human-voice.mjs` catches the ones marked **(script)**. The rest
need a read.

## Formatting

| Tell | Instead |
|---|---|
| Emoji anywhere **(script)** | Nothing. Let the words carry the warmth. |
| Em dashes and spaced en dashes **(script)** | A period, a comma, a colon or parentheses. |
| `**bold**`, `# headings` **(script)** | Plain text. Facebook and email show the asterisks. |
| `!!`, or three or more `!` in one message **(script)** | One at most. |
| Title Case headline as the first line **(script)** | Sentence case, or start with the point and drop the headline. |
| A run of hashtags **(script, look)** | None, or one or two that people really search. |
| Bullets for things that aren't a checklist **(script, look)** | A sentence. |

## Phrases **(script)**

"I hope this email finds you well", "I wanted to reach out", "circling back",
"don't hesitate to", "feel free to reach out", "delve", "tapestry",
"a testament to", "elevate", "seamless", "effortless", "unlock", "game-changer",
"in today's fast-paced world", "look no further", "it's important to note",
"embark on", "your wedding journey", "dive in", "navigate the world of",
"realm", "plethora", "myriad", "meticulous", "nestled", "boasts", "vibrant",
"bustling", "say goodbye to", "picture this", "ever wondered", "here's the
thing", "the best part?", "whether you're a...", "certainly!", "great
question!", "in conclusion", "moreover", "furthermore", "additionally,",
"one-stop shop", "take it to the next level", "curated", "bespoke".

## Shapes **(script for the first two)**

- **"It's not just X, it's Y." / "Not only X, but Y."** Just say Y.
- **"No X. No Y. Just Z."** Say Z plainly.
- **Everything in threes.** "Plan, organize and celebrate." Use the two that
  matter, or the four that are true.
- **Warm-up openers.** "Planning a wedding can feel overwhelming." Cut the
  first sentence and see if the message gets better. It usually does.
- **Summary closers.** "In short, Bridal Team makes planning easier." The
  reader just read it. Stop after the ask.
- **Question hooks.** "Ever wondered how much a DJ costs?" Say how much a DJ
  costs.
- **Same-length sentences.** Read it aloud. If it has a drumbeat, vary it.
- **Generic praise in outreach.** "I love your stunning work!" could be sent
  to anyone, which is how vendors know it was. Name one real thing, like "the
  Vizcaya rain-day shots", or leave praise out.
- **Hedging stacks.** "This may potentially help you possibly save." Pick one.
- **Over-politeness.** "I would be absolutely delighted if you might consider."
  "Want to claim it?" works better.
