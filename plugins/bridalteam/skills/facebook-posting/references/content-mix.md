# Content mix: 50 posts a month

## The mix

| Type | Per month | What it is | Link to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guide tip | 15 | One useful fact or step from a guide, written so it helps without clicking | That guide |
| Tool spotlight | 8 | One free tool and the one thing it works out for you | The tool page |
| Planning question | 7 | A question couples really ask, answered in 2–4 lines from a guide | The guide it comes from, or `/planner` ("ask your own") within the 3-a-month page limit |
| Conversation | 8 | A question for followers: "this or that", "what's the one thing…", "what are you stuck on?" | No link |
| Blog post | 5 | The main idea of one post in `lib/blog.ts` | That post |
| Seasonal | 4 | Timely planning: engagement season (December), booking season (January–March), summer weddings, holidays | A matching guide or tool |
| About us | 3 | Honest notes from a small team: what's new on the site, "what should we build next?" | The new feature, or none |

That's 50. With fewer slots, cut guide tips first.

Sources:
- Guides: `lib/guides.ts` (20 guides, each with `faqs` and `keywords`).
- Blog: `lib/blog.ts`.
- Tools: `/wedding-checklist`, `/wedding-budget-calculator`, `/wedding-rsvp`,
  `/wedding-seating-chart`, `/wedding-website`, and `/planner`.
- Ready-made feed posts: `marketing/facebook/<date>-feed-posts.md`, made by
  the `social-graphics` skill from every Pinterest pin, with 1080 × 1350
  images and checked captions. Use these first for guide and tool posts,
  photo posts before text graphics, and mark which ones a month uses.
- Images: the pins in `marketing/pinterest/` and the Story frames in
  `marketing/facebook/` already state their facts correctly.

## Spacing rules

- No three posts of the same type in a row.
- No page linked more than 3 times a month, or twice within 7 days.
- At least 1 post in 5 has no link (the conversation posts cover this).
- Each tool gets a spotlight at least once a month.
- Midday slots carry the guide and tool posts with their graphics; weekday
  evenings the conversation, question, blog, seasonal and about posts, each
  with an Unsplash photo.

## Format

- **Every post is a photo post** (`images` set). Facebook drops the link
  preview from photo posts, so any link goes at the end of the message. Put
  the hook in the first line, since Facebook cuts the text after about two
  lines.
- **Guide and tool posts** use an image that carries the fact: a pin, a
  Story frame, or a new image (1080 × 1350 PNG, under 1 MB, in the style of
  the pins: Jost and Raleway, brand orange `#f25e00`).
- **Conversation, question, blog, seasonal and about posts** use a free
  Unsplash photo that fits the topic (SKILL.md, "Photos").
- Length: 40–120 words. Short lines, lists with `•`.
- At most 2 hashtags, only if they're real searches (for example
  `#weddingplanning`). Usually none.
- At most one emoji per post, and only where it helps.

## Voice

- A friendly, practical planner talking to one couple. "You", not "brides".
  Couples of every kind plan weddings.
- Specific over hype. "Book the venue about 10 months out" beats "Planning
  made easy!"
- Free means free: say it plainly when a tool is free, no "limited time".
- No pressure tactics, fake urgency or "only X spots left".
- End conversation posts with a real question, and answer the replies.
