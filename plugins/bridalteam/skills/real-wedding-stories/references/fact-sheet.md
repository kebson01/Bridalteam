# Fact sheet: questions for a Real Weddings story

Ask these, and write down who answered. Anything unanswered stays a
`[bracketed question]` in the draft.

## Must have
- Couple's names, as they want them shown (full names, or first names only)
- Wedding date
- City
- Venue name
- Photographer or studio name
- Consent: has the couple agreed to the story and photos being public? Has
  everyone clearly shown agreed? Who said yes, when, and how (text, call)?

## Makes the story
- How they met, in a sentence or two
- One moment from the day they still talk about
- Why they chose the venue
- Colors or theme, if they had one
- Anything they'd tell a couple planning now

## Vendors (credit only what they confirm)
- Florist, DJ or band, caterer, cake, dress shop, hair and makeup, planner,
  officiant, rentals
- For each: is the business still open? A link if they have one
