# Finding public places where couples gather

Use web search: Facebook's own search isn't reachable from here. Swap in the
city or metro, starting with `LAUNCH_METRO` (`lib/site.ts`).

## Search terms

```
site:facebook.com/groups "engaged" "South Florida" 2027
site:facebook.com/groups "brides" Miami OR "Fort Lauderdale" OR "Boca Raton"
site:facebook.com/groups "wedding planning" Florida
site:facebook.com/groups "2027 brides"
site:facebook.com/groups "budget wedding" Florida
site:facebook.com/events "bridal show" OR "wedding expo" Florida
"engaged in Miami" OR "Miami brides" facebook group
```

Also look for occasion-specific groups that fit what Bridal Team hosts:
bridal showers, engagement parties, vow renewals, elopements and
destination weddings in Florida.

## What makes a good group

| Good sign | Bad sign |
|---|---|
| Couples ask real planning questions | Every post is a vendor ad |
| Posts in the last week | Last post months ago |
| Rules name a promo day or allow promotion with admin OK | "No promotion of any kind" (skip it, and record why) |
| Local to a metro we cover | National, with 200k members and no focus |

## What to record (Communities table)

Name, URL, public or private, size, the promo rule quoted word for word (or
`rule to confirm`), and the date checked. Never record members' names or
anything from their posts.
