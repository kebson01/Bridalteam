# Congratulations replies

Warm, specific congratulations for newly engaged couples. Started 2026-11-17
at the owner's request. They come in two parts:

- **Part A: replies on the Bridal Team Page.** For people who comment on one of
  the engagement call-out posts (Nov 27, Dec 30 and Jan 1, plus the Dec 1
  "Engaged this season?" post). They chose to talk to us, so a reply is welcome
  and allowed. This is workflow 4 in `../SKILL.md`.
- **Part B: personal notes in groups.** For the owner to post **as themself**,
  in Facebook groups they really belong to, where the group's rules allow
  comments like this. No brand, no link, no invitation. Part B exists only to
  be kind. If it starts turning into a sales channel, stop using it.

Everything in `../SKILL.md` still applies. Most importantly, Bridal Team never
comments on people's personal engagement posts, so Part B is never posted
from the Page. These are only starting points: change each one to fit what the
person actually said, and never post the same line twice in the same thread
or group.

## How to use them

1. Read what they wrote. Pick a line that fits (their season, city, how it
   happened), then add one detail of your own that shows you read it.
2. Use their first name if they gave it. Don't use pet names you weren't
   given, and don't guess at their partner's gender: "your partner", "you
   two" and "the two of you" work for every couple.
3. **Page replies only:** add a planning tip only if they asked a question or
   named a date or season. Add a link only when it helps, and use at most one,
   tagged `utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=comment&utm_campaign=<post folder name>`.
4. Never ask for their date, venue or contact details in private, and never
   send a DM unless they messaged the Page first.
5. Log the count in the Conversations table of `COUPLES-TRACKER.md`, never
   names.

  wonderful year of planning.

  family, all of it. Wishing you both a lifetime of happiness.

  hard to beat. Congratulations to you both!
- Congratulations! However it happened, the big planned moment or the quiet
  one, it's yours, and that's what makes it perfect.
- Wishing you both so much happiness. Congratulations, and thank you for
  telling us.

### With a tip, when they name a season or ask where to start

Facts in this section come from `lib/guides.ts` (`wedding-planning-checklist`,
`when-to-book-wedding-vendors`). Check them there before you change them.

- Congratulations! For a <season> wedding, the venue comes first, because it
  sets your date. After that, book the vendors who can only work one wedding
  a day: photographer, caterer, and band or DJ. Those fill up 9 to 12 months
  out.
- Congratulations to you both! If you'd like a head start, three things
  shape almost everything else: a budget, a rough guest count, and a date and
  venue. Everything else can wait until you've enjoyed this a bit.
- Congratulations! About 14 months is a comfortable planning window for most
  weddings, but less is completely doable. Start at the top of a checklist and
  go a little faster. The free one is here if it helps: <tagged
  /wedding-checklist link>
- Congratulations! When you're ready for a wedding website, you can make one
  on Bridal Team for free, with a private RSVP link for each household: <tagged
  /wedding-website link>. No rush at all.

Use the last two sparingly: no more than one in four replies should have a
link.

allow comments like this. Never mention Bridal Team here, never add a link,
and never follow up with a message.

- Congratulations! What a beautiful ring, and what a lovely way to tell
  everyone. Wishing you both all the happiness in the world.
- This made my day. Congratulations to you two!
- Congratulations! Enjoy being engaged. The planning will still be there next
  month.

  that feels exactly like the two of you.

  for years.

  about the date.
