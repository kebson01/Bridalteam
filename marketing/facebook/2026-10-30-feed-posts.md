posted.** There's no Facebook posting tool in Claude's sessions, so the owner

`marketing/pinterest/`. So every fact still comes from the page it links to.

## Before posting

- **Images:** `2026-10-30-feed/`. Each one is 1080 × 1350 JPEG, Facebook's
  4:5 feed size, with the full pin scaled onto a soft background so nothing
  is cropped. Files are named `<batch>-<pin>-<name>.jpg`.
- **Links:** use each URL exactly as written. They're tagged
  `utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_content=fb-<pin slug>`, so visits
  show in `page_events` with source `facebook`.
- **How to post:** in Meta Business Suite, choose **Create post**, add the
  image, paste the caption (the link is already at the end), and choose
  **Schedule**.
- **Timing:** one a day. The suggested dates below run Oct 31 to Dec 14. Use
  any time that suits the Page; 12:13 pm ET keeps them off the top of the
