## Posting steps

1. In the Facebook app, switch to the **Bridal Team Page**, not your
   personal profile, and tap **Create story**.
2. Select all six images **in order**.
3. On frames 2 to 6, add a **Link sticker** with the URL from the table and
   place it just under "Tap the link below to try it ↓". Each frame gets its
   own URL so you can see which tool people tap.
4. Share it to the Page's story. A story disappears after 24 hours, so add
   it to a **Highlight** on the Page to keep it, for example "Free tools".
