## Posting steps

1. In the Facebook app, switch to the **Bridal Team Page**, not your personal
   profile. Then tap **Create story**.
2. Select all six images **in order**. Each shows for about 5 seconds, and
   viewers can hold to pause.
3. On **frame 6**, add a **Link sticker** and place it just under "Tap the
   link below ↓", over the empty orange area. Use this URL:

   `https://bridalteam.com/planner?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=story&utm_content=story-1`

4. Optionally, add the same link sticker on frames 2–5 so viewers can open
   the matching guide. Use these URLs, so each guide's visits are counted
   separately:
   - Frame 2: `https://bridalteam.com/guides/save-the-dates-vs-invitations?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=story&utm_content=story-1`
   - Frame 3: `https://bridalteam.com/guides/wedding-planning-checklist?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=story&utm_content=story-1`
   - Frame 4: `https://bridalteam.com/guides/how-much-does-a-wedding-cost?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=story&utm_content=story-1`
   - Frame 5: `https://bridalteam.com/guides/who-pays-for-the-wedding?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=story&utm_content=story-1`
5. Share to the Page's story. A story disappears after 24 hours. To keep it,
   add it to a **Highlight** on the Page, for example "Planning tips".
