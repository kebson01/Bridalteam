# Photos for November 2026's text posts

The 20 weekday-evening posts (conversation, question, blog, seasonal and about)
were planned as text and link posts. On 2026-10-30 the owner asked for every
post to carry a picture, so each one got a free Unsplash photo, cropped to
1080 × 1350 (Facebook's 4:5 feed size). Posts that had a `link` now carry
the same tagged URL at the end of the message instead, since Facebook drops
the preview card from photo posts.

Every photo was found through the Unsplash API, is a free photo under the
Unsplash License (not Unsplash+), and was checked full size: no identifiable
faces, no legible personal names, no brand logos. Attribution isn't required;
it's recorded here so anyone can check where an image came from.
