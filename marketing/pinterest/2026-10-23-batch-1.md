# Pinterest pins: batch 1 (2026-10-23)

Drafted by the `couples-acquisition` skill. **Nothing here is posted.** The
owner reviews, edits and publishes each pin, then marks it `posted` in
`COUPLES-TRACKER.md`.

Every fact on a pin is quoted from the guide it links to (`lib/guides.ts`,
all updated 2026-07-22). If a guide changes, check its pin before reposting.

## Before posting

- **Business account and claimed website.** Pins from a claimed
  `bridalteam.com` carry the domain and the profile picture. That item is on
  the `seo-promotion` foundations list.
- **Boards.** Create these four once and reuse them:
  - *Wedding Planning Checklists & Timelines*
  - *Wedding Budget Tips*
  - *Wedding Etiquette & Invitations*
  - *Free Wedding Planning Tools*
- **Images are ready.** Each pin has a finished PNG in
  `2026-10-23-batch-1/`, named after the pin (for example
  `01-save-the-dates-vs-invitations.png`). They follow the format below.
- **Image format.** 1000 × 1500 px (2:3), PNG or JPG. Use brand colors: orange
  `#ff8c1c` or `#f36705`, near-black `#222`, white, and light gray for panels.
  Headline in Jost (bold), body in Raleway, the same fonts as the site. Put
  `bridalteam.com` small at the bottom of every pin.
- **Links.** Use the tagged URL exactly as written, so `page_events` can tell
  Pinterest traffic apart (`utm_source=pinterest`).
