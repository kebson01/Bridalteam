## Pin 1: Guest list tiers

- **Board:** Wedding Etiquette & Invitations
- **Title:** How to Make a Wedding Guest List Without the Drama
- **Description:**
  Set your number before the names, then sort everyone into three tiers:
  - immediate family and closest friends;
  - extended family and good friends;
  - coworkers and acquaintances.

  Need to cut? Cut from the bottom. RSVPs low? Invite the next tier. Pick one
  rule each for plus-ones, kids and coworkers, and apply it to everyone.
- **Alt text:** Three stacked tiers of a wedding guest list: closest family
  and friends, extended family and good friends, then coworkers and
  acquaintances.

## Pin 3: When to book wedding vendors

- **Board:** Wedding Planning Checklists & Timelines
- **Title:** When to Book Wedding Vendors: The Right Order
- **Description:**
  Book in this order to keep your first choices:
  - **9–12 months out:** the vendors who take one wedding per date
    (photographer, videographer, planner, caterer, band or DJ);
  - **6–8 months:** florist, cake, hair and makeup, officiant and rentals;
  - **2–4 months:** transportation, day-of stationery and favors.
- **Alt text:** Three groups of wedding vendors by when to book them: 9 to
  12 months, 6 to 8 months, and 2 to 4 months before the wedding.
- **Link:**
  `https://bridalteam.com/guides/when-to-book-wedding-vendors?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_content=book-vendors`
- **Image:** `03-when-to-book-wedding-vendors.png`

## Pin 5: Wedding registry rules

- **Board:** Wedding Etiquette & Invitations
- **Title:** 4 Wedding Registry Rules Guests Will Thank You For
- **Description:**
  1. Register before you send save-the-dates.
  2. Add about two gifts per guest across low, medium and higher prices.
  3. Mix in a cash, honeymoon or charity fund.
  4. Never print the registry on the invitation. Put the link on your
     wedding website instead.
- **Alt text:** Four numbered wedding registry tips: register early, about
  two gifts per guest, cash funds are fine, and never on the invitation.
- **Link:**
  `https://bridalteam.com/guides/wedding-registry-guide?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_content=registry`
