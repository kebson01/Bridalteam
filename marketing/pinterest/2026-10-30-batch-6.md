## Pin 1: Wedding video styles

- **Board:** Choosing Wedding Vendors
- **Title:** Cinematic or Documentary? How to Choose Your Wedding Video Style
- **Description:**
  - **Cinematic** films are polished and edited to music, like a short movie.
  - **Documentary** films play events out more fully, and in order.
  - **Or both:** many videographers deliver a tight 3–5 minute highlight film
    plus a longer edit.

  Before you book, watch a couple of complete films, not just highlight
  reels, and listen: are the vows and toasts crisp, or muddy and wind-blown?
- **Alt text:** A video camera on a tripod filming a wedding, above a list of
  wedding video styles: cinematic, documentary, or both.

## Pin 2: Filter venues first

- **Board:** Choosing Wedding Vendors
- **Title:** Choosing a Wedding Venue? Filter by These 3 Things First
- **Description:**
  Before you fall in love with a space, filter by the things you can't bend:
  your budget, your guest count and your date range. A stunning venue that
  seats 80 doesn't work for 150 guests, no matter how good the photos look.

  Then, on every tour, picture the actual flow: where guests park, where the
  ceremony becomes the reception, and what happens if it rains.
- **Alt text:** A garden gazebo decorated with flowers above rows of white
  ceremony chairs, with the headline "Filter venues by 3 things first".
- **Link:**

## Pin 3: Wedding photo styles

- **Board:** Choosing Wedding Vendors
- **Title:** Find Your Wedding Photography Style
- **Description:**
  Wedding photographers tend toward a style:
  - light and airy;
  - dark and moody;
  - classic and timeless;
  - documentary.

  None is better, but you'll live with it forever, so pick the one you
  genuinely love. Ask to see two or three complete weddings, not just a
  highlight reel, to check they're consistent.
- **Alt text:** A camera with a long lens beside pink flowers, above a list
  of four wedding photography styles.
- **Link:**
  `https://bridalteam.com/guides/how-to-choose-a-wedding-photographer?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_content=photo-styles`

## Pin 4: Florist consultation checklist

- **Board:** Choosing Wedding Vendors
- **Title:** What to Bring to Your Wedding Florist Consultation
- **Description:**
  Bring:
  - a few inspiration photos;
  - your color palette;
  - your venue details;
  - your budget;
  - a short list of flowers you love or want to avoid.

  Florists think in images, and a clear brief gets a far more accurate quote
  than "something pretty and seasonal."
- **Alt text:** A bride holding a bouquet of red and pink roses and dahlias,
  above a checklist of five things to bring to a florist consultation.
- **Link:**

## Pin 5: Minimoon or honeymoon

- **Board:** Wedding Planning Checklists & Timelines
- **Title:** Minimoon Now, Big Honeymoon Later?
- **Description:**
  A minimoon means leaving right after the wedding for a short trip, then
  taking the big honeymoon later. It lets you decompress without the pressure
  (and expense) of planning a major trip during an already busy time.

  There's no rule: do what fits your energy and budget.
- **Alt text:** A quiet tropical beach with overwater bungalows, with the
  headline "Minimoon now, big trip later?"
- **Link:**
  `https://bridalteam.com/guides/honeymoon-planning-guide?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_content=minimoon`
