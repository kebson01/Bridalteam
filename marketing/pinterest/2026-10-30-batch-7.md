## Pin 1: Save without regret

- **Board:** Wedding Budget Tips
- **Title:** 5 Ways to Save on Your Wedding Without Regret
- **Description:**
  Cut costs on things guests forget, and protect the things they'll
  remember:
  1. Marry on a Friday, a Sunday or in the off-season for lower venue rates.
  2. Trim the guest list before you trim vendors. It's the highest-leverage
     cut.
  3. Prioritize photography; it's what you keep long after the day.
  4. Choose in-season, local flowers over imported blooms.
  5. Skip favors most guests leave behind.

## Pin 2: Where couples overspend

- **Board:** Wedding Budget Tips
- **Title:** Where Couples Overspend on Their Wedding
- **Description:**
  The common budget-busters:
  - a guest list that quietly grows (catering, bar and rentals all scale per
    guest);
  - upgrades added late, like a bigger bar package or more florals;
  - the small line items, such as tips, postage, alterations and vendor
    meals, that add up fast.

  Set aside about 5% as a buffer from day one.
- **Alt text:** A long candlelit reception table with flowers, above three
  places couples overspend and a reminder to keep a 5% buffer.

## Pin 3: Questions for your DJ or band

- **Board:** Choosing Wedding Vendors
- **Title:** 6 Questions to Ask Your Wedding DJ or Band Before You Book
- **Description:**
  - Will the person we meet be the one performing on our date?
  - How do you handle must-play and do-not-play lists and guest requests?
  - What does setup need: space, power and how long?
  - Do you provide ceremony and cocktail-hour sound, and mics for toasts?
  - What's your backup plan if equipment fails or you're ill?
  - How much overtime is available, and at what rate?

## Pin 4: What a per-plate price includes

- **Board:** Choosing Wedding Vendors
- **Title:** Wedding Catering: What Does the Per-Plate Price Include?
- **Description:**
  A per-head quote can mean very different things. Confirm whether it covers
  staff, rentals (plates, glassware, linens), setup and cleanup, and service
  charges, or whether those are extra.

  Add up food, staff, rentals, bar, service charge and tax before you
  compare. The lowest per-plate number often isn't the lowest total.
- **Alt text:** A reception table with glassware, wine and flowers, above a
  checklist of what a catering per-plate price may or may not include.
