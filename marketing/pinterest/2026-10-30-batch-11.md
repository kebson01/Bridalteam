## Pin 1: Never book without a tasting

- **Board:** Choosing Wedding Vendors
- **Title:** Never Book Your Wedding Caterer Without a Tasting
- **Description:**
  Never book significant catering without tasting the actual menu. A tasting
  also shows how the food holds up at scale, and lets you fine-tune
  seasoning, portions and presentation. Bring anyone helping you decide, and
  ask how they handle dietary needs.
- **Alt text:** A plated dinner of sliced meat and roasted vegetables beside

## Pin 2: Your DJ is also your MC

- **Board:** Choosing Wedding Vendors
- **Title:** Your Wedding DJ or Band Is Also Your MC
- **Description:**
  Your DJ or bandleader usually runs the room: announcing entrances, cueing
  toasts and dances, and reading the crowd. That hosting skill matters as
  much as the music. Ask how they handle the flow of the night and requests,
  and ask for audio or video from an actual wedding, not just a promo clip.
- **Alt text:** A vintage microphone in front of blurred colored lights,

## Pin 3: Count who's in the chair

- **Board:** Choosing Wedding Vendors
- **Title:** Wedding Hair and Makeup: Count Who's in the Chair
- **Description:**
  Count everyone getting professional hair or makeup (you, attendants,
  mothers), because it drives both timing and cost. More people means an
  earlier start or a second artist. Confirm whether the quote is per person,
  and whether there's a minimum.
- **Alt text:** An ornate gold mirror on a white wall, above three things to
  check about how many people need hair and makeup.
- **Link:**

## Pin 4: Start with per-guest math

- **Board:** Wedding Budget Tips
- **Title:** Start Your Wedding Budget With Per-Guest Math
- **Description:**
  Guest count is the biggest lever in your entire budget. Catering, bar,
  rentals, invitations and favors all scale with the number of people.
  Cutting the list by 20 guests often saves more than trimming any single
  vendor. Before anything else, estimate your cost per head and multiply.
- **Alt text:** A large reception hall with long tables under string lights
  and chandeliers, above the costs that scale with guest count.
- **Link:**
  `https://bridalteam.com/guides/how-much-does-a-wedding-cost?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_content=per-guest-math`
- **Image:** `04-per-guest-math.png`
- **Photo:** https://unsplash.com/photos/a-large-room-filled-with-lots-of-tables-and-chairs-Rkj0ms67lio,
  by Jennifer Kalenberg, Unsplash License.

## Pin 5: Budget the honeymoon with the wedding

- **Board:** Wedding Planning Checklists & Timelines
- **Title:** Budget Your Honeymoon Alongside the Wedding
- **Description:**
  Decide the honeymoon budget alongside the wedding budget, not after: it's
  easy to spend everything on the day and leave nothing for the trip.
  Account for flights, lodging, food, activities and a cushion. A honeymoon
  registry or cash fund can help guests contribute.
- **Alt text:** An open suitcase with a straw hat and travel essentials,
  above the five things a honeymoon budget should cover.
- **Link:**
  `https://bridalteam.com/guides/honeymoon-planning-guide?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_content=honeymoon-budget`
