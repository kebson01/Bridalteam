## Pin 2: The wedding money talk

- **Board:** Wedding Budget Tips
- **Title:** How to Have the Wedding Money Talk With Your Families
- **Description:**
  1. **Talk before you book**, so you know the real total up front.
  2. **Ask for a fixed amount:** what each contributor is comfortable giving,
     not a blank check.
  3. **Ask what comes with it.** Money often comes with a say, like guest-list
     input, so decide together how much influence a contribution carries.
  4. **Keep one shared budget:** total funds in, costs out, and who's covering
     what.
- **Alt text:** A cup of coffee, a notebook and a pen on a wooden table, above
  four steps for talking about who pays for the wedding.

## Pin 3: Questions for your officiant

- **Board:** Choosing Wedding Vendors
- **Title:** 5 Questions to Ask Your Wedding Officiant
- **Description:**
  - Are you legally able to officiate in our location, and do you handle the
    license paperwork?
  - Do you personalize the ceremony, and can we review a draft?
  - Can we write our own vows, and will you help structure them?
  - Will you run the rehearsal?
  - How long does a typical ceremony run?

  The rules vary by location, so verify the specifics with your local
  marriage authority.
- **Alt text:** A wooden ceremony arch with flowers and draped fabric, above
