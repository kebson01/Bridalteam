## Pin 2: Questions for your florist

- **Board:** Choosing Wedding Vendors
- **Title:** 6 Questions to Ask Your Wedding Florist
- **Description:**
  - Have you worked at our venue, and are there any floral restrictions?
  - What's realistic for our budget, and where would you splurge or save?
  - Do you offer rentals (vases, arches, candles), or is everything
    purchased?
  - Can ceremony flowers be repurposed at the reception?
  - Who sets up and breaks down, and is that included?
  - What happens if a flower we chose isn't available that week?
- **Alt text:** Candlelit roses and carnations in glass vases, above six

## Pin 4: Split the guest list fairly

- **Board:** Wedding Etiquette & Invitations
- **Title:** How to Split the Wedding Guest List Fairly
- **Description:**
  If parents are contributing, they'll often expect input. Agree on how the
  list divides before invitations are drafted. A common split is thirds:
  one for the two of you and one for each set of parents.

  Then track RSVPs in one place, keeping names, meal choices and responses
  together, so your headcount, seating chart and caterer numbers all match.
- **Alt text:** A bright reception room with white tablecloths and a tall

## Pin 5: Keep the day on time

- **Board:** Wedding Planning Checklists & Timelines
- **Title:** 4 Ways to Keep Your Wedding Day on Time
- **Description:**
  1. **Pad the morning:** have everyone ready 30–45 minutes before you think
     you need to be.
  2. **Buffer the ceremony:** seating guests always takes longer than
     planned.
  3. **Space the toasts** between courses, not all at once.
  4. **Share the timeline a week ahead** with vendors, the wedding party and
     family, with names and phone numbers next to each hand-off.
- **Alt text:** An antique clock above a mantel covered in roses, above four
  tips for keeping a wedding day on schedule.
