## Pin 1: Save on wedding flowers

- **Board:** Wedding Budget Tips
- **Title:** How to Save on Wedding Flowers Without It Showing
- **Description:**
  1. Choose in-season, locally grown flowers. They cost less and look fresher.
  2. Spend on what people see up close: the bouquet, the ceremony backdrop
     and the centerpieces.
  3. Reuse ceremony arrangements at the reception.
  4. Bring your florist a visual brief.

  Flowers usually take about 8–10% of the budget.
- **Alt text:** Four ways to save on wedding flowers: in-season and local,
  spend where people look, reuse ceremony flowers, and bring a visual brief.

## Pin 2: DJ or band?

- **Board:** Choosing Wedding Vendors
- **Title:** Wedding DJ or Band? How to Choose
- **Description:**
  - **A DJ:** the widest range of songs, the exact recordings, and usually a
    lower price.
  - **A band:** live energy and spectacle, but it costs more, needs more
    space and plays a set repertoire.

  Or use both: a DJ for the reception and a soloist or trio for the ceremony
  and cocktails. Either way, they're also your MC.
- **Alt text:** Side-by-side comparison of a wedding DJ and a live band.
- **Link:**
  `https://bridalteam.com/guides/how-to-choose-a-wedding-dj-or-band?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_content=dj-or-band`
- **Image:** `02-dj-or-band.png`

## Pin 3: Catering service styles

- **Board:** Choosing Wedding Vendors
- **Title:** Wedding Catering Styles: Plated, Buffet, Family-Style or Stations
- **Description:**
  Pick the feel first:
  - **Plated** is formal and needs more staff.
  - **Buffet** is relaxed and lets guests choose.
  - **Family-style** sits in between and gets people talking.
  - **Stations** suit a lively, mingling reception.

  Then compare total cost. The lowest per-plate price often isn't the lowest
  total once staff, rentals and service charges appear.
- **Alt text:** Four wedding catering service styles, plated, buffet,
  family-style and stations, with what each is like.
- **Link:**
  `https://bridalteam.com/guides/how-to-choose-a-wedding-caterer?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_content=catering-styles`
- **Image:** `03-wedding-catering-styles.png`

## Pin 4: Wedding cake pricing

- **Board:** Wedding Budget Tips
- **Title:** How Wedding Cake Pricing Really Works (and How to Save)
- **Description:**
  - Wedding cakes are priced per slice. Sugar flowers, fondant, hand-piping
    and extra tiers all add to the cost.
  - Plan on about one slice per guest, but not all of it has to come from the
    display cake. A small cake for the cutting photo plus a plain kitchen
    sheet cake works.
  - Ask how it will hold up in your venue's heat.
- **Alt text:** Four facts about wedding cake pricing: per slice, about one
  slice per guest, a kitchen cake, and how heat affects buttercream.
- **Link:**
  `https://bridalteam.com/guides/how-to-choose-a-wedding-cake?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_content=cake-pricing`
- **Image:** `04-save-on-the-wedding-cake.png`

## Pin 5: Who can officiate

- **Board:** Choosing Wedding Vendors
- **Title:** Who Can Officiate Your Wedding?
- **Description:**
  You can be married by:
  - a religious leader;
  - a professional celebrant;
  - a civil officiant, such as a judge or registrar;
  - in many places, a friend or family member who gets temporarily
    authorized.

  The rules vary by location, so check who may officiate and the license
  timing with your local marriage authority, early.
- **Alt text:** Four kinds of wedding officiant, with a reminder that the
  rules vary by location.
- **Link:**
