## Pin 2: How many invitations to order

- **Board:** Wedding Etiquette & Invitations
- **Title:** How Many Wedding Invitations to Order (and the Costs People Forget)
- **Description:**
  Order by household, not by guest: one invitation per couple or family.
  Count your households, then add 15–20 extras for mistakes, keepsakes and
  last-minute additions. Reprinting a small batch later costs far more per
  piece.

  Budget for the extras too: postage (bulky or square envelopes cost more),
  RSVP return postage, and any inner envelopes, belly bands or wax seals.
- **Alt text:** Sealed envelopes with gold wax seals, above the rule of one
  invitation per household plus 15 to 20 extras.
- **Link:**

## Pin 3: Invitation wording

- **Board:** Wedding Etiquette & Invitations
- **Title:** Wedding Invitation Wording: Traditional or Modern?
- **Description:**
  Formality is a spectrum:
  - **Traditional wording** spells everything out: "request the honour of
    your presence".
  - **Modern wording** is warmer and plainer: "together with their families,
    invite you to celebrate".

  Match the wording to the wedding's tone, and be consistent across every
  piece.
- **Alt text:** An envelope with a red wax seal and a seal stamp, above
  traditional and modern invitation wording side by side.

## Pin 4: Hair and makeup questions

- **Board:** Choosing Wedding Vendors
- **Title:** 6 Questions to Ask Your Wedding Hair and Makeup Artist
- **Description:**
  - Is a trial included, and when should we schedule it?
  - Do you come to us, and is there a travel fee?
  - How long do you allow per person, and do you bring an assistant for
    larger groups?
  - Do you use long-wear or airbrush products for photos and heat?
  - Can you do touch-ups later, or provide a touch-up kit?
  - What's the deposit and cancellation policy?
- **Alt text:** Makeup brushes, lipstick and compacts laid flat, above six

## Pin 5: Make the cake tasting count

- **Board:** Choosing Wedding Vendors
- **Title:** How to Make Your Wedding Cake Tasting Count
- **Description:**
  Taste before you commit, and bring your color palette and a photo or two of
  styles you like. Ask how the cake holds up in your venue's temperature,
  since buttercream can soften in summer heat. Then ask:
  - Are delivery and setup included?
  - Can you accommodate allergies or a gluten-free tier?
  - When do you need the final design and guest count?
- **Alt text:** A white tiered cake with berries and peonies, above a
  checklist for a wedding cake tasting.
