## Pin 1: Questions to ask a venue

- **Board:** Choosing Wedding Vendors (new)
- **Title:** 7 Questions to Ask Every Wedding Venue Before You Sign
- **Description:**
  Bring these to every venue tour and write the answers down:
  1. What's included, and what costs extra?
  2. Is catering in-house?
  3. What's the rain or heat backup plan?
  4. How many hours does the rental cover, and what are the overtime fees?
  5. When can vendors set up and when must they be out?
  6. What are the deposit and cancellation policy?
  7. How many restrooms are there, and is the site accessible?
- **Alt text:** Seven questions to ask a wedding venue, from what's included
  to restrooms and accessibility.
- **Link:**
  `https://bridalteam.com/guides/how-to-choose-a-wedding-venue?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_content=venue-questions`

## Pin 2: Questions to ask a photographer

- **Board:** Choosing Wedding Vendors (new)
- **Title:** 6 Questions to Ask Your Wedding Photographer Before Booking
- **Description:**
  Before you book, ask:
  - Will you shoot our wedding yourself? Is there a second shooter?
  - How many hours of coverage, and what's overtime?
  - When do we get the photos, and about how many?
  - Do we get full printing and sharing rights?
  - What's your backup plan for gear failure or illness?
  - Have you shot at our venue or one like it?

  And look at two or three full galleries, not just highlights.
- **Alt text:** Six questions to ask a wedding photographer, including who
  shoots, hours of coverage, delivery and image rights.
- **Link:**
  `https://bridalteam.com/guides/how-to-choose-a-wedding-photographer?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_content=photographer-questions`
- **Image:** `02-wedding-photographer-questions.png`

## Pin 3: What goes on a wedding invitation

- **Board:** Wedding Etiquette & Invitations
- **Title:** What Goes on a Wedding Invitation (and When to Send It)
- **Description:**
  Every invitation needs:
  - the hosts and the couple's names;
  - the date, start time and ceremony location;
  - reception details;
  - how to RSVP and by when;
  - any dress code;
  - your wedding website.

  Mail them about 8 weeks before, set the RSVP deadline 3–4 weeks before, and
  order one per household plus 15–20 extras.
- **Alt text:** Checklist of what goes on a wedding invitation, with mailing
  about 8 weeks before and the RSVP deadline 3 to 4 weeks before.
- **Link:**
  `https://bridalteam.com/guides/wedding-invitations-guide?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_content=invitation-checklist`
- **Image:** `03-what-goes-on-a-wedding-invitation.png`

## Pin 4: Hair and makeup timeline

- **Board:** Choosing Wedding Vendors (new)
- **Title:** Wedding Hair and Makeup: Plan the Morning Backward
- **Description:**
  - Allow 45–60 minutes per person in the chair, plus a 30-minute cushion,
    because it always runs long.
  - Whoever is photographed getting ready goes last, so their look is freshest.
  - Book the trial near your date, so hair length and skin match.
- **Alt text:** Wedding hair and makeup timing: 45 to 60 minutes per person,
  a 30 minute cushion, the photographed person last, and a trial near the
  date.
- **Link:**

## Pin 5: Honeymoon in 5 steps

- **Board:** Wedding Planning Checklists & Timelines
- **Title:** How to Plan Your Honeymoon in 5 Steps
- **Description:**
  1. Pick the kind of trip first, then the place.
  2. Set the honeymoon budget alongside the wedding budget, not after.
  3. Book flights and lodging early.
  4. Renew passports that expire within six months of travel.
  5. Consider a minimoon now and the big trip later.

  And tell hotels and airlines you're newlyweds.
- **Alt text:** Five numbered honeymoon planning steps, from choosing the kind
  of trip to taking a minimoon.
- **Link:**
