# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [TODO: publication date — set when finalized]**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Bridal Team, LLC** ("Bridal Team," "we,"
"us"), a Florida limited liability company, collects, uses, and shares
information when you use the Bridal Team website and applications (the
"Service"). By using the Service you agree to this Policy.

## 1. Information we collect

**Information you provide:**
- Account details (name, email, password credentials, profile photo).
- Planning content (weddings, tasks, budgets, timelines, guest/travel details,
  saved inspiration, messages to the AI planner).
- Vendor details (business name, category, location, gallery, contact info).
- Community content (posts, comments, reviews, group memberships).
- Payment information for vendor subscriptions (processed by Stripe — we do not
  store full card numbers).
- Communications you send us (support, waitlist signups).

**Information collected automatically:**
- Device and usage data (IP address, browser type, pages viewed, actions taken),
  and cookies/local storage needed to run the Service and keep you signed in.

**Information from third parties:**
- If you sign in or interact through a third-party service, we may receive
  limited information from it.

## 2. How we use information

- Provide, maintain, and secure the Service and your account.
- Generate AI planning assistance from the prompts and context you provide.
- Process vendor subscriptions and prevent fraud.
- Communicate with you (transactional email, and — only with consent where
  required — updates).
- Enforce our Terms and comply with legal obligations.
- Improve the Service, using aggregated or de-identified data where feasible.

## 3. Legal bases (where applicable, e.g., EEA/UK)

We process personal data to perform our contract with you, for our legitimate
interests (operating and improving the Service, security), to comply with law,
and with your consent where required.

## 4. How we share information

We do **not** sell your personal information. We share it only:
- With service providers/sub-processors (Section 5) who process it on our behalf.
- With other users when you post or share content publicly (e.g., vendor
  profiles, community posts, shared weddings).
- For legal reasons (to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, or protect
  rights, safety, and the Service).
- In a business transfer (merger, acquisition, or asset sale), subject to this
  Policy.

## 8. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards (including
access controls and database row-level security) to protect personal data. No
method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export,
delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object or withdraw
consent.
